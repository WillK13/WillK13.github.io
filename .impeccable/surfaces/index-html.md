# Surface brief: Magellan research companion

## Scope

- Primary target: `index.html`
- Visitor mode: Read
- Audience: SC26 judges and systems and sustainability researchers.
- Job: understand the paper's contribution, inspect its strongest results, and open the full evidence.
- Content: paper title, author and university, concise abstract, mechanism summary, verified headline results, four supplied evaluation figures, and the poster PDF.
- Constraints: preserve experiment qualifications; do not invent claims; keep the page simple, responsive, accessible, and visibly academic rather than commercial.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A camera-ready paper companion that lets the research and figures lead, refusing the company-site pattern of a promotional hero, feature cards, and conversion copy.

OWN-WORLD: Crisp white paper, carbon ink, graphite rules, and one restrained deep teal evidence color. Square scholarly panels, generous margins, running labels, serif reading text, and plain sans-serif metadata. A small globe with one orbital arrow is the sole illustrative signature.

STORY: The reader identifies the paper and author, reads the contribution in a short abstract, scans the best verified measurements with their experiment context, understands the decentralized mechanism, then inspects every supplied figure and the poster.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow masthead places the Magellan globe mark at upper left and quiet section links at right. Below, the exact paper title occupies the main column, followed by Will Kaminski and Tufts University; an abstract begins immediately, with a compact key-results strip visible at the viewport edge. No marketing CTA.

FORM: Camera-ready proceedings front matter translated into a responsive research microsite; grounded direction 6 of 7; seed:cab85fa445ec9d30.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
